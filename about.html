Who We Are

Our Mission

The Glenn High School English Honors Society cultivates a passion for literature and language, promotes academic excellence, and encourages members to contribute to the community through literacy-focused service projects.

We believe that the ability to read critically and write powerfully are among the most transformative skills a person can develop — and we exist to nurture exactly that in every member.

Programs & Activities

What We Do

🌙

Reading Nights

Monthly reading nights where students and local authors share work, discuss themes, and celebrate the written word together.

📦

Book Drives

We organize community book drives to ensure every child in our area has access to quality reading materials and stories.

🎤

Poetry Slams

Students perform original poetry in a supportive, competitive environment that builds confidence and creative expression.

🏫

Tutoring & Workshops

Members volunteer as writing tutors and facilitate creative writing workshops at nearby elementary and middle schools.

🏅

Writing Contests

Short-story and essay competitions with prizes give members a platform to showcase their finest literary work.

🎙

Author Talks

We host guest speakers — published authors, poets, and journalists — to inspire and connect students with literary careers.
