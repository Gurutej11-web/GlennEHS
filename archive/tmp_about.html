About the English Honors Society

Inspiring excellence, creativity, and leadership through language and literature.

Our Mission

The Glenn High School English Honors Society cultivates a passion for literature and language, promotes academic excellence, and encourages members to contribute to the community through literacy-focused service projects.

What We Do

Reading Nights

We host monthly reading nights where students and local authors share work and discuss themes in literature.

Community Outreach

Members organize book drives, volunteer tutoring, and creative writing workshops at nearby schools.

Competitions & Events

We run poetry slams, short-story contests, and host guest speakers to expose students to diverse literary careers.

History, Goals & Membership

History

Our chapter supports student creativity and community literacy through regular events, service projects, and partnerships with local schools and libraries. Over the years we've organized book drives, tutoring programs, guest-author visits, and student-led workshops that help members develop leadership and communication skills. Many alumni credit EHS with giving them early experience organizing community-facing projects and pursuing further study in literature, education, and journalism.

Goals

We strive to encourage literary growth, foster leadership, and provide recognition for academic achievement in English, while also creating opportunities for students to practice public speaking, editing, and event planning.

Membership

Membership is open to students who meet the chapter's academic criteria and agree to participate in meetings, service activities, and at least one project per year. Prospective members can find details and the application process on the Events page when the chapter opens recruitment.
